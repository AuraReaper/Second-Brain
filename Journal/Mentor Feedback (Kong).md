## 🎯 High-Level Action Items

- **Container Orchestration:** Master Docker and Kubernetes.
- **CI/CD Automation:** Build pipelines using GitHub Actions and ArgoCD.
- **Prometheus Stack:** Learn custom metrics, scraping, and dashboards.
- **OpenTelemetry:** Implement full distributed tracing, context propagation, and analyze performance bottlenecks via span metrics.
- **Secure Coding Principles:** Study the OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities, understand data protection/PII masking paradigms (at rest and in transit), and master access management protocols (cloud RBAC and IRSA configurations).
- **System-Level Security:** Study structured threat modeling frameworks (like STRIDE). Practice mapping out data-flow diagrams (DFDs) for new features and identifying external attack vectors before writing code.

### 🤝 Soft Skills

- **Timeboxing:** When executing unfamiliar technologies, frame your research requests as formal, 2-to-3-day spikes. Define your discovery goals upfront to keep stakeholders perfectly aligned on timelines.
- **Asynchronous Visibility:** Cultivate a daily habit of sharing quick, bulleted progress updates in team Slack channels. Making your incremental structural wins visible builds immense trust before a PR is even opened.
- **Proactive Alignment:** Instead of waiting for a mentor to hand down architectural boundaries, schedule proactive 30-minute whiteboarding alignment sessions as soon as you identify a conceptual gap or design ambiguity.
- **Cross-Team Collaboration:** Start interfacing directly with product managers, security teams, and cross-functional infrastructure units to translate high-level business goals into practical technical roadmaps.
